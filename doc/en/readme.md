# Calculia

**Web application for practicing math and logical reasoning with short, visual activities.**

---

## What is Calculia?

Calculia is a digital tool for practicing math and logical reasoning at your own pace, in the browser, free of charge.

The app offers **15 activities** organized into **2 families**: Mathematics (numbers, operations, measures, money) and Reasoning & logic (patterns, sequences, riddles, the wallet, the clock, stories, what doesn't belong?, puzzle). See the full activity-by-activity catalogue in [`activities.md`](activities.md).

---

## Key features

### ✅ Designed for autonomy

- **Easy Reading**: short sentences, everyday vocabulary, one idea per screen
- **No pressure**: no timers, no negative scores, no punishment
- **Positive reinforcement**: celebrates correct answers with friendly sounds and animations
- **Progressive stars**: only ever added, never subtracted

### ✅ Accessible to everyone

- **Large buttons**: minimum 64×64 pixels
- **Large text**: clear readable font (Atkinson Hyperlegible)
- **High contrast**: light colours on a white background
- **Audio only where it adds value**: a 🔊 button appears only when the activity design requires it (e.g. hearing a number, listening to a riddle)

### ✅ In two languages

- 🇪🇸 **Español** (default)
- 🇬🇧 **English** (can be changed from the menu)

## Getting started

### 1. Open the app

Visit **[calculia.apptonomia.uk](https://calculia.apptonomia.uk)** or open `site/index.html` from a local server. The full step-by-step with **four ways to open Calculia** (internet, ZIP, Python, Node.js) lives in [`quick-guide.md`](quick-guide.md).

### 2. Choose an activity

On the main screen you'll see a grid of activity cards grouped by family (Mathematics / Reasoning & logic). Tap the activity you want to start.

### 3. Change language

Tap the language button (🇪🇸 or 🇬🇧) at the top of the screen.

---

## Example of use

Imagine you want to practise telling the time. Open the **🕐 Clock** activity. The app shows you an analogue clock and asks you to read the time. You can:

1. **Look at** the clock face
2. **Tap** the right answer
3. **Listen** to the question again with the 🔊 button

If you get it right, a brief celebration appears. If not, you get an encouraging message: "Almost. Try again!".

## Featured activities

### 🔢 Mathematics

| Activity | What it does |
|----------|--------------|
| **Numbers** | Read, write and compare whole numbers, sequences and place value. |
| **Math tables** | Practise multiplication and division facts through repetition and short challenges. |
| **Mental math** | Quick calculation exercises with the four operations, without writing intermediate steps. |
| **Money** | Recognise coins and banknotes, count amounts, work out totals. |
| **Wallet** | Practise giving and receiving money, working out change and managing a small budget. |
| **Quantities** | Compare quantities, estimate and reason about "more / less / equal". |
| **Roman numerals** | Read and write Roman numerals up to the thousands. |
| **Fractions and measures** | Recognise, compare and operate with fractions and common units of measure. |
| **Temperature** | Read a thermometer, compare temperatures and convert between °C and °F. |

### 🧩 Reasoning and logic

| Activity | What it does |
|----------|--------------|
| **Riddles** | Verbal reasoning: read a short clue, infer the answer. |
| **Patterns** | Detect and continue visual and numerical patterns. |
| **Odd one out** | Identify which element does not belong in a set, and explain why. |
| **Puzzle** | Spatial reasoning: rearrange pieces to rebuild a picture or a sequence. |
| **Clock** | Read the time on analogue and digital clocks, and reason about durations. |
| **Stories** | Short story problems: extract the relevant data and choose the right operation. |
